Planning & arrival

What riding experience do I need?+

Welcome to the Desert is for motorcyclists with little or no off-road experience. For Level 2 tours, you should already have experience riding off road. Tell us honestly about your experience so we can help you find the right tour and group.

Explore Welcome to the Desert ↗
How old do I need to be?+

You can travel and participate independently from age 18. Under 18s need parental permission and an accompanying adult. Motorcycle licence requirements still apply.

How do I get there?+

Ouarzazate is our base for desert tours. Fly into Ouarzazate or travel via Marrakech, around a three-hour transfer away. Coastal tours are based around Agadir. Check airports and travel dates with us before booking flights, especially for tours with different start and finish points.

Check your travel plans with us ↗
How do airport transfers work?+

Transfers are not normally included in the tour price. We help organise them and can often combine the journey with other riders. If you fly directly into Ouarzazate, we collect you ourselves. We agree the connection and price before your trip.

Bike & riding gear

What do I need to bring?+

Essential documents

  • Full motorcycle licence (valid at the destination)
  • Passport, valid for at least 6 months
  • Travel and medical insurance, including motorcycling cover

Recommended riding gear

  • Helmet (mandatory)
  • Enduro / off-road boots
  • Gloves
  • Body armour and knee protection
  • Goggles or glasses — we recommend goggles because of the dust
  • Hydration pack or CamelBak

If you don't have your own riding gear, we can arrange rentals — but we must know well in advance! We'll always make sure we've got something in your size, but if too many people need the same size and we need to grow our gear selection, we need time.

Personal kit

  • Lightweight clothing for the evenings
  • Fleece or jacket — it can get a little cold, even in the desert
  • Sunglasses, sunscreen, lip balm
  • Toiletries and any medication you need
  • Small day bag or backpack
  • Device chargers — Morocco and the Balkans use European two-pin sockets, so bring a plug adapter if you need one

We provide a 30-litre duffle bag for your personal items and extra gear during the tour, while your main travel bag stays back at our base — space in the support 4×4 is limited. Waterproofs are rarely needed; we monitor the weather before your tour and let you know if they'd be a worthwhile inclusion.

Can I rent riding gear?+

Yes. Helmets, boots and body armour can be reserved in advance. Current daily prices are listed on the bikes page. Let us know your sizes early so we can have suitable gear ready.

What happens if I damage the bike?+

Scratches and scuffs are part of riding and tolerated. Broken parts are charged at parts price. A damage deposit of €600 / £500 applies.

On the ride

How far and how long do we ride each day?+

Most days cover between 120 and 250 kilometres, depending on the route and terrain. Open desert pistes can mean longer distances; technical mountain trails and sand can mean shorter days. Expect five to seven hours including photo, refreshment and lunch stops. We adapt the pace to the group; individual tour pages describe the daily stages.

What if I have dietary requirements?+

Please let us know early so we can coordinate with the accommodation and try to meet your requirements. Arranging this at short notice is much harder.

Do I need cash?+

Morocco uses the dirham (MAD). Do not rely on card payments everywhere along the route. Most medium-sized towns have cash machines. Many costs are already included in your tour price; by agreement, we can also cover extra expenses and settle up at the end.

How do you prepare for conditions in Morocco?+

We monitor local conditions and adjust routes accordingly. Our guides know the terrain and local arrangements. Keep valuables secure, respect local customs and follow your guide's advice while riding.
