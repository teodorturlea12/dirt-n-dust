Privacy Policy

The short version: we collect what we need to answer you and run your tour, we don't track you, and we never sell your data. The full version follows.

Last updated: 16 August 2026

1. Who we are

Dirt N Dust Limited (Company No. 16834019), registered office: Brynwyddog, Llanbister, Llandrindod Wells, Powys, LD1 6TL, United Kingdom. We run guided enduro tours and are the controller for the personal data described here. You can reach us at hi@dirtndust.tours or +44 7387 146717.

2. What data we collect and why

We only collect what we need to answer your enquiry and run your tour:

  • Enquiries (email, WhatsApp, phone, enquiry form): your name, contact details and the content of your message — used to reply and to plan your tour. Legal basis: steps prior to entering a contract (UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) and our legitimate interest in responding to you.
  • Booking details (via our Google booking forms): the information requested in the form, such as name, date of birth, nationality and contact details — used to organise your booking. Legal basis: performance of a contract.
  • Technical data (server logs): IP address, browser type and pages visited, processed by our hosting provider to deliver the website securely. Legal basis: legitimate interest in a secure, working website.

3. Cookies and analytics

This website does not set tracking cookies and does not use analytics services. If that changes, this policy will be updated first and any consent required will be asked for before anything is set.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. We share it only with service providers we need to operate:

  • Our website hosting provider (server logs, technical delivery).
  • Google (only if you fill in one of our Google booking forms — Google's own privacy policy applies).
  • WhatsApp / Meta (only if you choose to contact us via WhatsApp — Meta's own privacy policy applies).
  • Tour partners on the ground where strictly needed to run your tour (for example accommodation bookings).

5. International transfers

If you use Google Forms or WhatsApp, those providers may process data outside the UK/EEA under their own safeguards (such as the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses).

6. How long we keep data

Enquiry correspondence is kept as long as needed to deal with your request. Booking records are kept for the duration of the contract and any legal retention periods (typically six years for accounting records in the UK), then deleted.

7. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us at any time for:

  • Access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction of inaccurate data.
  • Deletion of your data, where no legal duty requires us to keep it.
  • Restriction of processing or objection to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • A copy of the data you gave us in a portable format.

8. Complaints

If you think we handle your data incorrectly, contact us first at hi@dirtndust.tours — we will sort it out. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK supervisory authority (ico.org.uk).

Questions about your data? Write to hi@dirtndust.tours.
