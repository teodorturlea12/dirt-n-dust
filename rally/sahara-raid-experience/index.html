01The rally

Test, train and taste the real thing.

Proudly Moroccan — made by Moroccans for the rest of the world, with members of the Wilderness Wheels team in organisational roles.

New format

This year the rally introduces roadbook navigation and a timed class — the perfect place to test, train and gain experience for bigger rallies, while the original categories stay for those who want a raid experience.

Real rally life

Two real rally bivouacs, a marathon stage, a mass start and dunes are all part of the 2026 edition. Comfort is minimal.

A class for everyone

Enough categories to give every participant a shot at the podium:

  • Roadbook
  • GPS
  • Single-cylinder
  • Multi-cylinder
  • Vets 40+
  • Classic
  • Ladies

02The week

Eight days in the desert.

From scrutineering to the finisher's medal — the shape of the rally week.

12–13 Dec

Bivouac opens

Arrival of participants, then admin checks and technical scrutineering.

14 Dec

Shakedown and prologues

Shakedown, then the raid and rally super-stage prologues to set the starting order.

15–18 Dec

The stages

Four days of riding between 150 and 300 km, including a mass start and a marathon stage between two real rally bivouacs. Stage 4 on the 18th ends with the prize giving — podiums, finisher's medals and the end-of-rally celebration.

19 Dec

Departure

Pack up, say your goodbyes and start planning the next one.
