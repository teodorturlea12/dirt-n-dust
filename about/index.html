Who we are

Riders first. Company second.

Dirt N Dust was established in 2025 — but the team behind it has spent seven years guiding riders through Morocco's deserts, mountains and coastlines. We built the company we wanted to ride with: small groups, properly prepared bikes and routes we know stone by stone.

01How we ride

Small groups, full support, no herd.

Most of our bookings are private tours — a group of mates, their dates, their pace. Public departures run the same way: capped at ten riders, never anonymous.

Small by design

Ten riders is the hard ceiling. Below that, the guide knows how everyone rides by day one — and the route adapts to the group, not the other way round.

Supported properly

Desert tours run with a 4×4 support vehicle, a mechanic and luggage transfer. You carry a hydration pack; everything else meets you at the night's stop.

Honest material

Every photo on this site was shot on our tours. What you see — the dunes, the camps, the bikes — is what you ride.

02The team

The people behind the dust.

A small core crew runs the tours, with partner guides and mechanics joining per tour. These are the faces you'll actually ride with.

Chief of bikes & making shit happen

Jack

Jack is the one who keeps Dirt'n'Dust running, from organising the chaos to fixing whatever the bikes throw at us and leading the way when things get technical. We strongly suspect there's engine oil running through his veins. He somehow always looks like he's either just come back from an adventure or accidentally started one. This Welsh lad started riding bikes at five, before most of us could even confidently ride a bicycle, though his earliest moto memory is crashing a Puch Magnum X into a hedge. He started guiding enduro tours in the UK way back at the tender age of 20. After enough years of British rain and much time on the African continent, he eventually realised there are places where riding doesn't involve being permanently wet. The Moroccan desert has been his second home ever since.

One hand on the camera, one on the throttle

Vanessa

Vanessa makes sure Dirt'n'Dust doesn't just happen, but actually gets seen and remembered. While others avoid riding with a backpack, she casually carries several kilos of camera gear like it's part of the guiding kit. A trip to Dakar in winter 2024 eventually led to her teaming up with Jack, running tours and sharing her love for the desert with others. She handles the storytelling and marketing behind Dirt'n'Dust and somehow makes joining the next adventure feel like the only sensible thing to do.

Mechanic, support driver & helping hand

Amine

Amine is our helping hand on site, good friend and one of those people you can always count on. If you happen to see him without a smile on his face, you know something is seriously going wrong. Luckily, that doesn't happen very often. Growing up in Agadir, he loved meeting people from all over the world through summer jobs, through this discovering a passion for mechanics, off-road riding and racing. After earning his mechanics diploma and completing several internships, he joined an off-road tour company, gaining valuable experience working in the desert, so in due course, joining Dirt'N Dust felt like a natural step when great friends share passions. He's very ambitious, whether it's as a mechanic, on the bike or when he jumps behind the wheel of the support car, or occasionally working for pro teams at prestigious rally events. Basically, if something needs doing, Amine is already halfway there.

Rally support & partner in crime

Christoph — Aquila Rally Sport

Christoph is from Austria and has a suspicious talent for making questionable ideas sound extremely reasonable — hence he's currently preparing to race Dakar in 2029. He started Aquila Rally Sport to make riding rallies more accessible: rally transport and support across Europe, getting bikes to the start line and handling the logistics so riders can focus on the part they actually came for. We partner with Christoph for events, rallies and passion projects — including the Sahara Raid Experience.
