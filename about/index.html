Who we are

The people behind the tours.

Dirt N Dust was established in 2025 — but the team behind it has spent seven years guiding riders through Morocco's deserts, mountains and coastlines. We built the company we wanted to ride with: small groups, properly prepared bikes and routes we've ridden for years.

How we ride

Small groups, full support.

Most of our bookings are private tours — a group of mates, their dates, their pace. Public departures run the same way: capped at ten riders.

The team.

Chief of Making Shit Happen

Jack

Jack is the one who keeps Dirt’N’Dust running, from organising the chaos, to fixing the bikes and leading the way, not only on tours but also when things get technical. We strongly suspect there's engine oil running through his veins. He somehow always looks like he's either just come back from an adventure or is accidentally starting one. This Welshman first hopped on a motorcycle at the age of four, before most of us could even confidently ride a bicycle, though his earliest moto memory is crashing a Puch Magnum X into a hedge. By the age of 20, he was guiding trail tours in the UK. After years spent in the British rain and extensive exploratory trips across the African continent, he came to a realisation: there are places where you don't have to get soaked every time you ride a bike. The Moroccan desert has been his second home ever since.

After realising that there were ways the enduro tour industry could be drastically improved in Morocco, Jack decided that setting up Dirt’N’Dust was only sure-fire way to move things forward.

The backup when things go wild

Lili

Lili keeps the team and the riders in shape, on and off the bike. As a physiotherapist and trained medical first aider, she makes sure a sore back, a twisted wrist or a bad fall doesn't end the adventure. The German spent two years travelling full-time through Asia and came back knowing a bit of everything, with one rule: if something needs doing, learn it and do it. She's also a helping hand wherever and whenever needed. When the day gets long, the sand gets deep or the nerves kick in, she is the one to talk to, ideally before the crash rather than after. Either way, she'll keep you laughing while patching you up, so you barely notice the pain.

One Hand on the Camera, One on the Throttle

Vanessa

Vanessa ensures that Dirt’N’Dust leaves a lasting impression. While others might avoid riding with a backpack, she carries several kilos of camera gear as a matter of course, as if it were an essential part of her standard guiding equipment. Following a trip to Dakar in the winter of 2024, the Swiss native teamed up with Jack; joining him to guide tours and share their passion for the desert and motorcycles with others. Vanessa handles the storytelling and social media for Dirt’N’Dust, making the decision to join the next adventure seem like the only logical choice.

An Eye for Detail & a Hand for Help

Amine

Amine is our helping hand on the ground, a good friend and someone you can always rely on. He ensures that, should the need arise during our tours, support is on hand immediately. He serves as a support driver and mechanic, ready to handle any necessary repairs. If you ever happen to see him without a smile on his face, you can be certain that something is wrong, though fortunately that almost never happens. He grew up in Agadir, and after earning his mechanics diploma, he decided to support off-road tour companies and eventually ended up joining Dirt’N’Dust. He loves side quests, so he occasionally works for pro teams at prestigious rally events. That's probably where his get-things-done mentality comes from, especially when it has to happen fast.

Rally Support & Partner in Grime

Christoph — Aquila Rallye Sport

Christoph has a suspicious talent for making questionable ideas sound extremely reasonable. The Austrian is therefore currently preparing for his goal of racing in the Dakar Rally in 2029. He serves as our partner in organising rally support and various other events. Through his project, Aquila Rallye Sport, he has also launched his own initiative aimed at making rallies accessible to a broader audience. When it comes to transport for rallies or logistical support across Europe, Christoph is the ideal person for the job. He ensures that riders can focus completely on what they love most, riding, while everything else required for a seamless operation is handled behind the scenes.
