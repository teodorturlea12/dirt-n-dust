4 Day Desert Raid

Four riding days from the rocky passes of the Anti-Atlas to the dunes of Erg Chegaga. A desert loop from Ouarzazate, with the bike, meals, accommodation and support taken care of.

  • Level 2 of 3 · 85% off-road · 5 nights
  • Ouarzazate → Zagora → Erg Chegaga → Foum Zguid → Ouarzazate

€2,200per rider

Dates & availabilityTalk to Jack →

Trade the countdown for the dunes.

Cross winding canyons and dry riverbeds before reaching the remote dune sea of Erg Chegaga. Spend the night at a desert camp, then ride out across Lac Iriki and the old Paris–Dakar pistes. Four days on the bike, a different landscape each day.

The ride,
day by day.

From the first briefing to the last ride back to base. Distances are approximate; the route adapts to the conditions and the group.

  1. Arrival

    Meet in Ouarzazate

    Settle in, meet your guides and fellow riders, then share dinner and a briefing for the days ahead.

  2. Day 1Approx. 250 km

    Ouarzazate to Zagora

    Ride east through the Anti-Atlas, over a mountain pass and into the canyons. Old villages and rocky pistes lead to Zagora, a town with deep Paris–Dakar roots.

  3. Day 2Approx. 130 km

    Zagora to Erg Chegaga

    Start with the views from Jbel Zagora, follow the old rally tracks south and turn west into the dunes. After reaching camp, head out for dune practice before dinner.

  4. Day 3Approx. 120 km

    Erg Chegaga to Foum Zguid

    Ride the dunes in the cool of the morning, then leave on a former Paris–Dakar piste. Cross Lac Iriki, salt flats and open desert tracks on the way to Foum Zguid.

  5. Day 4Approx. 200 km

    Foum Zguid to Ouarzazate

    Head north through gorges and mountain tracks in the Anti-Atlas. Stop for lunch in Agdz, then ride back to Ouarzazate for a shower and the final dinner.

  6. Departure

    The journey home

    Say goodbye to the team. We can help arrange your onward transfer from Ouarzazate; airport transfers are charged separately.

Before you ride.

Is this the right level for me?+

This is a level 2 of 3 tour, with around 85% off-road riding and a mix of rock, riverbeds and sand. Tell Jack about your experience so he can help you decide whether this ride or Welcome to the Desert suits you better.

Can I have my own room?+

The package uses shared twin rooms. The single room supplement is €175, subject to availability.

Can I add a training day?+

An extra riding day before the main tour may be arranged for training or getting familiar with the bike. Ask Jack to confirm availability and the additional price.
