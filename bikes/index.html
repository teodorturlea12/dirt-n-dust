02Setup & support

Set up for you, backed on the piste.

Tell us your height, weight and experience when you book and your bike is set up before you arrive — then a mechanic and 4×4 travel with the desert tours.

Setup options

  • Lowered bike for shorter riders
  • Bar risers for taller riders
  • Stiffer suspension for riders above 100 kg

Scratches and scuffs are part of riding and tolerated. Broken parts are charged at parts price, against a damage deposit of €600 / £500.

We don't normally rent bikes without a guide and support crew — if you have something specific in mind, ask.
