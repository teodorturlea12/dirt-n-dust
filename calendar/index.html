4 Day Desert Raid

+

Four riding days from Ouarzazate to Zagora, Erg Chegaga and Foum Zguid, returning through the Anti-Atlas. Level 2 of 3, around 85% off-road.

  • Prepared KTM or GasGas 450, fuel, third-party insurance, guide, 4×4 support, mechanic and luggage transport.
  • Five nights in shared twin rooms; breakfast, lunch and dinner. Single room supplement: €175, subject to availability.
  • Flights, airport transfers, riding travel insurance, alcohol and bike damage are excluded. An extra training day may be arranged separately.

Arrival and departure are included in the listed travel dates. For New Year: arrive 28 December, ride 29 December–1 January, depart 2 January.

Agadir–Dakar / Dakar–Agadir

+

  • A €300 refundable deposit gives you the first opportunity to sign up. Arrange it directly with the team.

Sahara Raid Experience

+

Roadbook stages, bivouac nights and dunes around Tan-Tan, Morocco. Four stages of 150–300 km, a mass start and a marathon stage. Dirt N Dust and Aquila Rally Sport offer bike, transport and support packages.

  • Entry fees paid to the organiser: Classic Raid €500 (GPX), Roadbook Raid €600 (untimed digital roadbook), Rally Race €700 (timed, with racing tracker).
  • All classes include bivouac access and food, medical and mechanical assistance, civil organisation insurance, a finisher’s medal and event T-shirt.
  • Entry excludes flights, bike transfer, navigation instruments, personal racing insurance, the bike and its insurance, and fuel.
  • Fly & ride €1,800: rally-prepared KTM 450 EXC-F, navigation, suspension setup, rally mousses and large tank; mechanical support, guidance, Agadir airport transfers and tent/sleep setup.
  • Own-bike transport from central Europe: basic €1,100, with your own bivouac setup and maintenance; full support €1,600, including mechanical services, tent/sleep setup and Agadir airport transfers.
  • Extras: full protective gear €150, workshop access €150, tent/sleep setup with basic transport €100.

Entry fees and bike/support packages are separate. Ask Jack to confirm the complete package for your chosen class.
